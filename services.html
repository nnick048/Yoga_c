Our Accessible Class Options

We provide a thoughtful selection of classes specifically tailored to meet the needs of all physical abilities, emphasizing absolute safety, community support, and personal comfort.

Core Class Types

  • Gentle Yoga: Ideated specifically for absolute beginners and older adults. This class focuses on very slow stretches, structural alignment, and deep breathing.
  • De-Stress Vinyasa Flow: Perfect for busy professionals looking to let go of work tension. This class links gentle movement patterns with continuous breath to build strength.
  • Restorative Yoga: Utilizes specialized props to fully support the physical body, allowing for prolonged relaxation and deep mental rest.

Private One-on-One Instruction

Receive individual instruction custom-tailored to your unique personal wellness goals, custom mobility modifications, or strict scheduling limitations. These private appointments can be arranged directly by speaking with our desk staff during regular hours.
