Our Mission and Inclusive Philosophy

Founded explicitly on the core principles of community inclusivity and physical accessibility, Riverbend Yoga Studio was built to provide a supportive local haven where anyone can experience the therapeutic benefits of yoga without fear of judgment, pressure, or intimidation.

Experience Our Supportive Community Atmosphere

Our Commitment to Accessibility Accommodation

We are completely dedicated to making our physical studio space welcoming for every person who walks through our doors. Our facility features completely zero-step entryways, wide paths, and we fully supply specialized yoga props including stable balance blocks, flexible extension straps, and sturdy chairs to guarantee every single practice is perfectly adapted to your personal physical requirements.

Studio Safety and Mindful Policies

To safely maintain the quiet, peaceful environment of our collective sanctuary, we kindly request that all students arrive at least ten minutes prior to their scheduled class time, completely silence all personal cellular devices upon entry, and leave outdoor shoes in the reception storage cubbies.
